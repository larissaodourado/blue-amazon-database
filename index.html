MARINE NATURAL PRODUCTS BRAZIL · AMAZÔNIA AZUL
01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Exploring marine biodiversity
through chemical knowledge.

The IARA Database is a scientific initiative dedicated to organizing information on natural products reported from organisms associated with Brazil's Amazônia Azul.

By bringing together data from scientific literature, the database aims to facilitate access to chemical structures and associated biological and taxonomic information, providing a resource for research and computational studies in natural product discovery.
